PHAN 4: HUONG DAN QUAN LY DANH MUC SACH & SAN PHAM (CATALOG & EXCEL)
================================================================================
Truy cap: /Admin/Product

1. Tao moi san pham / sach:
   - Nhan nut "Them San Pham Moi".
   - Nhap Ma SKU (duy nhat toan he thong, vi du: BK-VH-001).
   - Barcode: Co the nhap tay barcode co san tren sach hoac tick chon
     "Tu dong sinh Barcode" (He thong sinh chuan EAN-13: ABCyyMMxxxxxx).
   - Thong tin an pham sach: Nhap ISBN, Tac gia, Nha xuat ban, Nam XB, So trang,
     Ngon ngu, Hinh thuc bia (Bia mem, Bia cung...).
   - Nhap Gia von (Cost Price) va Gia ban (Selling Price).
   - Nhap Nguong canh bao ton kho toi thieu (Low Stock Threshold).
   - Nhan "Luu San Pham".

2. Xuat du lieu ra Excel:
   - Nhan nut "Xuat Excel" (/Admin/Product/ExportExcel).
   - File Excel (.xlsx) tao boi ClosedXML se tu dong tai ve, chua day du danh sach
     san pham, gia von, gia ban, ton kho hien tai va chi tiet xuat ban.

3. Nhap du lieu hang loat tu file Excel (Import):
   - Nhan nut "Tai file Excel mau" de lay form chuan 14 cot.
   - Dien thong tin sach vao file theo dinh dang mau.
   - Chon file va nhan "Tien hanh Nhap Excel".
   - He thong tu dong kiem tra hop le tung dong. Neu co dong bi loi (thieu SKU,
     gia am, sai dinh dang), he thong se thong bao ro so dong thanh cong va
     cho phep tai ve file "Bao cao loi chi tiet" co chu thich nguyen nhan loi.

PHAN 5: HUONG DAN QUAN LY KHO HANG & SO CAI KEP (DOUBLE-ENTRY LEDGER)
================================================================================
Truy cap: /Admin/Inventory

1. Xem ton kho thoi gian thuc (Stock Balances):
   - Xem so luong ton kho cua tung san pham tai tung kho chi tiet.
   - Canh bao mau do/vang doi voi cac mat hang co so luong ton <= nguong toi thieu.

2. Tra cuu So cai Giao dich Ton kho (Audit Trail):
   - Xem lich su chi tiet tai muc "Nhat ky Giao dich".
   - Moi dong ghi nhan ro: Thoi gian, Loai bien dong (Ban tai quay, Ban online,
     Nhap NCC, Chuyen kho, Kiem ke), Ma chung tu, So luong thay doi (+/-),
     So du ton kho truoc khi giao dich, So du ton kho sau khi giao dich va Nguoi thao tac.

3. Kiem ke & Dieu chinh ton kho (Stock Adjustment):
   - Nhan nut "Tao Phieu Dieu Chinh".
   - Chon Kho, San pham can kiem ke.
   - Nhap so luong ton thuc te kiem dem duoc va Ly do dieu chinh.
   - He thong tu dong tinh so luong chenh lech thua/thieu va ghi nhan bu tru
     vao so cai kep ma khong cho phep sua truc tiep so ton kho.

PHAN 7: HUONG DAN DIEU CHUYEN HANG HOA LIEN CUA HANG (INTER-STORE TRANSFER)
================================================================================
Truy cap: /Admin/Inventory -> Tab "Dieu chuyen kho"

Quy trinh 3 buoc dam bao khong that thoat hang hoa:
- Buoc 1: Lap phieu dieu chuyen (Trang thai: Draft)
  Chon Kho xuat (vi du: Kho Tong Central) va Kho nhan (vi du: Kho Quay Q1).
  Them cac san pham va so luong can chuyen.
- Buoc 2: Xuat kho van chuyen (Trang thai: InTransit)
  Nguoi quan ly nhan "Xac nhan Xuat kho".
  So luong ton tai Kho xuat se lap tuc bi tru qua so cai kep.
  Hang hoa duoc xep vao trang thai "Dang di duong" (In-Transit).
- Buoc 3: Nhan hang & Nhap kho dich (Trang thai: Received)
  Cua hang nhan kiem dem hang thuc te va nhan "Xac nhan Nhap kho".
  So luong ton tai Kho nhan duoc cong tang tren so cai kep.
  Phieu chuyen kho hoan tat 100%.

PHAN 8: HUONG DAN BAN LE TAI QUAY POS (KEYBOARD WEDGE, TICH DIEM, VIETQR)
================================================================================
Truy cap: /POS/Terminal
(Luu y: Thu ngan phai co Ca lam viec dang Mo moi duoc phep ban hang)

1. Thao tac ban hang nhanh:
   - Quet ma vach: Su dung may quet ma vach USB (hoat dong che do gia lap ban phim).
     Dua dau doc vao barcode tren bia sach, may quet tu dong tim san pham va
     them ngay vao don hang voi so luong mac dinh la 1.
   - Tim kiem thu cong: Go ten sach, ma SKU hoac barcode vao o tim kiem.
   - Chinh sua gio hang: Thay doi so luong, xoa dong san pham nhanh bang 1 click.

2. Khach hang & Tich diem thanh vien:
   - Nhap so dien thoai khach hang vao o "Tim khach hang" va an Enter.
   - Neu khach cu: Hien thi ten, hang thanh vien (Standard, VIP) va so diem hien co.
   - Neu khach moi: An nut "Them nhanh" de tao ho so khach hang trong 5 giay.
   - Tich diem: Moi 10.000d mua hang tu dong tich 1 diem thuong vao tai khoan.
   - Tieu diem: Nhap so diem muon su dung de giam gia (1 diem = 1.000d khau tru).

3. Thanh toan da phuong thuc (Split Payment):
   - Tien mat (Cash): Nhap so tien khach dua, he thong tu dong tinh tien thua tra khach.
   - Chuyen khoan VietQR: Chon phuong thuc VietQR, he thong tu dong hien thi Ma QR
     dong kem logo Ngan hang, so tai khoan va chinh xac so tien can thanh toan kem
     noi dung chuyen khoan duy nhat theo Ma don hang.
   - The tin dung/ATM (Card): Nhap ma chuan chi pos the vao he thong.
   - Thanh toan ket hop: Cho phep khach tra mot phan tien mat va phan con lai chuyen khoan.

4. Hoan tat don & In hoa don:
   - Nhan "Hoan tat Thanh toan". He thong tu dong tru ton kho quat qua so cai kep,
     cong diem thuong cho khach va tang doanh thu tien mat trong ca cua thu ngan.
   - Hoa don ban le kho giay 80mm chuan POS tu dong hien thi san sang de in.

PHAN 9: HUONG DAN MO CA, DONG CA & XUAT BAO CAO THU NGAN (SHIFT RECONCILIATION)
================================================================================
Truy cap: /POS/Shift

1. Mo ca dau ngay (Opening Shift):
   - Thu ngan chon Quay thu ngan (POS Register).
   - Nhap so tien mat dau ca duoc ban giao lam quy tien le (Opening Cash Float,
     vi du: 1.000.000d).
   - Nhan "Xac nhan Mo ca".

2. Theo doi trong ca:
   - Trong suot thoi gian ca mo, he thong tu dong ghi nhan va phan loai:
     + Tong doanh thu ban hang trong ca.
     + Doanh thu Tien mat.
     + Doanh thu Chuyen khoan VietQR.
     + Doanh thu The ngan hang.
     + So tien mat ly thuyet phai co trong ket = Tien dau ca + Doanh thu tien mat.

3. Dong ca cuoi ngay & Kiem dem chenh lech (Closing Shift):
   - Thu ngan dem toan bo tien mat thuc te co trong ket va nhap vao o "Tien mat thuc te".
   - He thong tu dong tinh Chenh lech = Tien mat thuc te - Tien mat ly thuyet.
   - CHINH SACH KIEM SOAT GIAN LAN:
     Neu chenh lech (thua hoac thieu) vuot qua nguong cho phep la 50.000d, he thong
     BAT BUOC thu ngan phai nhap "Ly do / Giai trinh chenh lech" truoc khi cho phep dong ca.

4. Báo cáo & Thong bao tu dong:
   - Xuat file Excel bien ban dong ca ClosedXML (/POS/Shift/ExportExcel/id).
   - Gui Email thong bao tu dong kem so lieu chenh lech toi hop thu Ke toan / Quan ly.
   - Gui tin nhan thong bao canh bao tuc thi toi nhom Quan ly tren Telegram.

PHAN 10: HUONG DAN QUAN LY CHI PHI (OPEX), BAO CAO P&L & TINH LUONG NHAN VIEN
================================================================================
Truy cap: /Admin/Finance

1. Quan ly Chi phi van hanh (OPEX):
   - Truy cap /Admin/Finance.
   - Nhan "Lap Phieu Chi".
   - Chon Cua hang, Danh muc chi phi (Thue mat bang, Tien dien nuoc, Internet,
     Vat tu dong goi, Marketing...).
   - Nhap So tien chi, Nguoi nhan tien va Dien giai chung tu.
   - Chi phi sau khi lap se duoc tinh ngay vao bao cao tai chinh cua chi nhanh.

2. Bao cao Ket qua Kinh doanh Lai/Lo (Profit & Loss - P&L):
   - Truy cap /Admin/Finance/ProfitLoss.
   - Chon Thang/Nam va Cua hang can xem (hoac chon "Toan bo chuoi").
   - He thong tinh toan chinh xac theo nguyen ly ke toan:
     (1) Doanh thu thuan ban hang (Net Revenue).
     (2) Gia von hang ban (Cost of Goods Sold - COGS) lay tu so cai tai thoi diem ban.
     (3) Loi nhuan gop (Gross Profit) = Doanh thu - Gia von. Ty suat Loi nhuan gop (%).
     (4) Chi phi van hanh (OPEX) chi tiet theo tung hang muc.
     (5) Loi nhuan hoat dong (Operating Profit) = Loi nhuan gop - Chi phi OPEX.
   - Cho phep xuat toan bo bang P&L ra file Excel ClosedXML chuan bao cao quan tri.

3. Tinh Luong nhan vien & Hoa hong ban le:
   - Truy cap /Admin/Finance/Payroll.
   - Chon Cua hang va Ky luong thang.
   - Cong thuc tinh luong:
     Luong thuc lanh = (Luong co ban / 26 * Ngay cong thuc te)
                       + Hoa hong ban hang (1% tren tong doanh so POS cua nhan vien)
                       + Phu cap an trua, xang xe
                       - Khau tru bao hiem & phat.

PHAN 14: XU LY SU CO THUONG GAP & LUU Y VAN HANH PRODUCTION

1. Su co loi chuoi ket noi SQL Server:
   - Kiem tra lai chuoi ket noi "ABCBookDb" trong file appsettings.json.
   - Dam bao SQL Server hoac LocalDB dang chay. Co the kiem tra bang lenh:
     sqllocaldb info mssqllocaldb

2. Su co may quet ma vach khong nhan:
   - Kiem tra che do cua may quet ma vach (phai o che do USB HID / Keyboard Wedge).
   - May quet phai duoc cau hinh tu dong them ky tu "Enter" (Suffix Carriage Return)
     sau moi lan quet ma de he thong tu dong kich hoat them vao gio hang.

3. Thay doi cau hinh Thong bao Email / Telegram cho moi truong Production:
   - Mo appsettings.json hoac su dung User Secrets / Environment Variables:
     "Notification:Telegram:BotToken": "dien_token_that",
     "Notification:Telegram:ChatId": "dien_chat_id_that",
     "Notification:Email:SenderEmail": "email_gui@congty.com",
     "Notification:Email:SenderPassword": "mat_khau_ung_dung"

4. Sao luu & Phuc hoi Co so du lieu:
   - Thiet lap lich Backup hang ngay cho database ABCBookShopDb tren SQL Server Agent.
   - File log duoc ghi tu dong vao thu muc "logs/abcbookshop-yyyyMMdd.txt" bang Serilog
     va luu giu toi da 14 ngay gan nhat.
